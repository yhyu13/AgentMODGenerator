Where should this art pipeline live?

This is the fork that decides everything else. Click one, then reply in the terminal.

A

In-repo image backend

Add an ImageClient protocol next to the existing LLM client. MiniMax is the first provider. Current SDV generators (ItemSpritesGenerator, UIAssetsGenerator, TextureGenerator) call it instead of _make_png().

Smallest change. Art is a dependency of generators, not its own pipeline.

B

Generic art pipeline (recommended)

A game-agnostic stage: ArtSpec → generate → post-process → AssetManifest. Image models (MiniMax first) are swappable providers. SDV is the first adapter that maps specs to Content Patcher paths + EditImage.

Matches “generic” in your ask. Logic pipeline stays; art becomes a parallel track with its own contract.

C

Standalone art service

Separate package/service. Prompt in, zip of sprites/UI/maps out. This repo only HTTP-calls it. No SDV knowledge inside the art service.

Most reusable, most work, overkill until a second game pack exists.
